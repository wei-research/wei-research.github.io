Short Biography

Wei Liu is a Professor in Artificial Intelligence, and the Director of Future Intelligence Research Lab, at the School of Computer Science, University of Technology Sydney (UTS), Australia. Wei obtained his PhD degree in Machine Learning at the University of Sydney (USYD). His current research focuses on LLM robustness, model hallucination, and multimodal machine learning.

Wei's research papers are constantly published in CORE A*/A and Q1 (i.e., top-tier) journals and conferences. He has received three Best Paper Awards. Besides, one of his first-authored papers received the Most Influential Paper Award in the CORE A Ranking conference PAKDD 2021. He has obtained and led significant amounts of research funding and projects, including ARC DP, government competitive grants, and industry research projects.
